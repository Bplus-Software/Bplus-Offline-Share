B+ Share 1.1.14, centered audio/playback device-test pre-release

Changes
- Seek line is centered vertically in the 50dp audio row, with no upward bottom-margin offset. Play/options share that center. Compact time remains below the line inside the row. Drawn mic and short voice cards retained.
- Playback remains a plain seek line. Earlier marked dashes may mean a waveform, but that interpretation has not been confirmed, so no waveform-style playback bar is silently substituted. Recording live amplitude bars remain.
- Playback sessions are lazy: no MediaPlayer/file source is created for each card during rendering. Source setup/preparation runs off the UI thread after Play. Only one note plays at a time. Progress polling stops on pause/end/release; elapsed text updates only when its second changes, with smoother 80ms seek progress while playing. A pending load that loses focus to another note does not auto-start later.

Lag diagnosis
- Source review confirmed avoidable main-thread source setup for every audio card, per-card progress loops continuing while paused, and simultaneous playback allowed. Those are real workload/overlap defects addressed here. The exact cause of the owner's reported audio stutter is NOT established without device traces/audio reproduction; provider access, decoding or device behavior could still contribute. No claim that changing a polling interval alone fixes audio.

Validation and limits
- Clean build, versionCode19, original signer, 304 EN/AR keys. Source guards cover zero line offset, background source setup, active-only progress, one active note and second-only clock updates. Voice/receipt/release-choice/gesture/record/path/share/group/nearby/message/updater suites pass. Exact released 1.1.13 candidate downloader redirect/SHA harness included.

Actual 1.1.14 pixels, audio smoothness, seek/pause/rapid card switching and Android provider/decoder performance remain unverified. Test-only, risk unmeasured. Need a phone recording plus the user's assessment of sound before promotion.
